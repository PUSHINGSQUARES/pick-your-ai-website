## Selected users and mobile context

The primary design user is an individual developer planning a small inference workload, including a local AI enthusiast considering existing hardware. The selected job is to retain and understand a decision while moving between a phone and the actual inference environment. This is a scope choice based on R1–R4, not a measured preference for mobile work.

A secondary, later user is a reviewer of a supplied decision record. Cross-device exchange, shared records and team approval are deferred until their accepted formats, authority and privacy requirements are available. Enterprise procurement, organization administration, multi-user governance, training operations and agent execution are not first-slice promises.

Mobile requirements follow from the chosen job: a person may be interrupted, may lose connectivity and may resume after the app is stopped. A saved acknowledgement must name durable local content. Pending edits, a missing pinned artifact and a newer local writer need distinct recovery states. These are engineering requirements, not claims that research has measured how often they occur.

The client may display a desktop accelerator, remote server or API option without asserting that the phone supplies its RAM, accelerator, runtime or credentials. Client device properties must not silently fill inference-target fields. Missing target memory, route, region, latency and workload assumptions stay missing.

## Connectivity, device and privacy constraints

Saved local work must remain usable without an account or network connection. Cached facts need exact versions and as-of/provenance context; caching is permitted only for the accepted source-use scope. Uncached content and server-only work must be visibly unavailable. Reconnection cannot silently replace a draft's pinned inputs.

A local draft can contain sensitive workload notes even when its catalog is public. The selected scope therefore excludes automatic upload, silent cloud backup claims, advertising identifiers and third-party collection. Private notes, credentials and user-specific metadata must not enter future exports or diagnostics without the agreed safe projection and explicit review. The app cannot promise that ordinary text entry detects every possible secret.

The current diagnostic's SDK/package choices are implementation evidence, not a qualified product/device matrix: package ai.pickyour.android.preview, minimum API 26 and compile/target API 35, unsigned debug artifact and disabled release variants at the pinned source below. #34/#47/#53/#54 retain product identity, supported-device, publisher and current platform assessment work. Nothing in S1 treats that Android device as the inference runtime.
